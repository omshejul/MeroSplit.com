MeroSplit

Track shared expenses with friends, split bills, and see who owes whom.

MeroSplit helps small groups keep track of shared expenses. Use it with friends, roommates, or people you travel with.

Add a bill, choose who was part of it, and split it evenly or with custom shares. MeroSplit shows who owes whom so everyone can see the same balances.

Keep shared bills clear

• Track shared expenses in groups
• Split bills evenly or with custom shares
• See who owes whom
• Record payments made outside the app, such as cash or bank transfers
• Invite people to a group. The group organizer approves new members.
• Review a Splitwise CSV export before importing it. Imports have size limits.
• Attach an image or PDF of a bill (optional)
• Add a profile picture (optional)
• Turn on alerts for new bills (optional)
• View your groups and the people you share expenses with on the Groups and People screens

Your records

Your records are saved to your account, so the group sees the same history. Recent data is kept on your phone, so you can view it and make changes without a connection. Pending changes are sent when you are back online. If a change conflicts with someone else's, MeroSplit shows it to you for review.

Payments happen outside MeroSplit

• It does not send, receive, or process payments. Payments happen outside the app and you record them in MeroSplit.
• It does not convert between currencies.
• It does not read text from receipt images.
• It does not automatically merge data from older versions stored on your phone.
