Privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026. MeroSplit is operated by Om Shejul. For privacy questions, contact mail@merosplit.com.

What you choose to save

MeroSplit uses a signed-in account. Your groups, people, bills and payment records are saved on the server. Your phone or supported regular browser keeps account-specific copies for offline use and queues offline changes to sync automatically when connected. Older records saved only on a phone are added to an account only after you confirm; signing in does not claim them.

We store your account email, short-lived protected sign-in challenge digests and delivery status, member names and identifiers, group membership, bills, amounts, dates, splits, notes, payment records and change history. We also store an optional UPI address, profile photo and bill attachments when you add them. A Splitwise CSV import saves the records and choices you confirm in its preview.

Why we use this information

We use these records to calculate balances, sync your account, manage invitations, display bill evidence, let members acknowledge payment records, provide exports and operate the service. Group members can see the group's records. Anyone holding a valid invitation link can see the invited person's involved bills, notes, balance, repayments and involved names on the invitation page without signing in. Share links only with intended recipients. Manual share-link joining requires a request and the organizer's approval. Email invitations reveal no ledger before the intended recipient signs in with a code sent to that exact address; verified recipients can then join that shared group. Typing a contact email does not link an account or send an invitation. A new bill that financially includes an unclaimed person may send a generic notification to the supplied address. That sign-in link verifies the mailbox only. Access to the bill still requires the organizer’s explicit invitation.

MeroSplit does not connect to your bank, read bank transactions or verify payments. A supported UPI link can open a separate payment app, where you review and authorize payment. UPI addresses and payment records are entered by people. Your saved UPI address is visible to linked members of your shared groups. Recording a payment does not send money. Opening or returning from a payment app never records payment automatically.

Bill conversations

Signed-in group members can read and post plain-text messages about a bill. We store these messages, their authors and times, unread status, chat policy acceptance, chat-only blocks and private abuse reports. Notifications go only to current people financially included in the bill, excluding the sender. Account email reminders batch unread messages after ten minutes. Generic email and push notices contain no message text. Eligible unclaimed contacts may receive an immediate generic notice that grants no access. Merely fetching a conversation does not mark it read. Blocking hides that sender's chat for you and stops future chat notices from them. It does not change shared financial records. Authorized support operators may review reports and hide abusive chat. Chat is not end-to-end encrypted. Your account export includes your chat facts, and deleting your account removes messages you authored.

Photos, files and notifications

Photo and file access begins when you choose to add a photo, attachment or import file. The API processes the file you upload, resizes images and removes image metadata before saving the optimized file in private object storage. This is not a guarantee that information visible in a photo or PDF has been redacted.

We keep account-level unread bill notices and the exact bill revisions you have seen. Expense updates in Me → Notifications control expense push and email across your devices. Phone permission controls only that device's push route; we store its installation identifier and push token linked to your account and session. Unseen bills may produce a generic push after 30 seconds. Optional email reminders arrive when a new bill is still unseen after 10 minutes, even if you use another screen or have no mobile app. Viewing the current bill on web or mobile cancels its pending reminder. Updates that are due together are grouped, and an already emailed notice is not repeated. Existing accounts keep their choice. New accounts start with email reminders on only when their sign-in screen discloses this; older clients without that disclosure start with reminders off. You can turn reminders off in Me. Alerts contain no bill amounts, descriptions or names; private push routing uses account, group, bill and notice identifiers. Online opt-out suppresses pending work. An offline change waits for retry, and a request already handed to a provider can still arrive. Sign-out revokes that device's registration. Account deletion removes preferences, notices and reminder delivery records. Permission changes are checked when the app becomes active.

If a new bill includes a supplied email without usable group access, a generic notification can be sent immediately. Adding or editing the contact alone sends nothing. Each email has a Stop bill emails link with an explicit confirmation. We retain a keyed mailbox preference to honor that choice, including after account deletion. Existing verified account email and expense-update OFF settings also suppress these emails. We do not infer app installation from notification tokens.

Connected agents

Connect MCP is optional. You sign in with the existing browser flow and explicitly approve each agent connection. An agent can read allowlisted ledger records and use selected bill/payment-record commands under your existing account and group permissions. Payment tools never send or verify money. We store the client name, approved scope, credential digests, expiry, usage time and revocation state. Disconnect in Me, Connect MCP revokes both access and refresh credentials. A request already running can finish. Account deactivation or deletion stops access. Connected agents process the records you authorize under their own policies.

Service providers and sharing

Railway hosts our API and PostgreSQL database. Resend delivers requested sign-in codes and explicit email invitations, plus unread expense reminders when enabled and generic new-bill notices for supplied mailboxes. Cloudflare R2 stores uploaded media privately. Expo, Apple and Google process push notifications and the device identifiers needed to deliver them. These providers process information to run the service. We do not sell your personal information, display ads or use advertising trackers in this version.

HTTPS protects information in transit. Group records are accessible to authorized members; media uses temporary access links. MeroSplit does not offer end-to-end encryption. The service may retain technical request and error information needed to operate and diagnose it.

Retention and deletion

Account and group records remain while needed for your use of MeroSplit. You can export your account or group records and delete your account from Me. Deletion removes your user account, sign-in sessions, email, UPI address and device registrations. Your uploads, including shared bill attachments, are removed from visible records and marked for physical deletion. New download links are blocked immediately. Previously issued links expire within five minutes; successful physical deletion removes the file earlier. Bill descriptions, notes and other text attributable to you are removed or replaced with neutral placeholders in current records and change history. Groups you own without another active linked member are removed.

Other members retain anonymous participant identifiers, numeric bill amounts, splits, dates and payment history so their balances remain correct. Content entered by other members remains theirs; contact us if it contains personal information about you that you want removed. A storage-provider failure can delay physical file deletion; we keep deletion markers so cleanup can be retried. Previously downloaded copies cannot be recalled. Deletion from the active service does not remove copies other people previously exported or downloaded. This beta currently has no configured scheduled database backups or point-in-time recovery.

Request account or personal-data deletion, including if you no longer have the app. Requests may require confirmation of account ownership. Deleting an account also clears its cached records and queued changes from the phone where you confirm deletion. Older phone records that you have not added to an account remain separate and can be removed by clearing the app's local data.

Browser storage

Regular supported browsers retain downloaded records, drafts and pending changes in site storage. Clearing site data removes those local copies. When a browser cannot open persistent storage, including some private windows, MeroSplit uses a temporary online-only tab. Unsent changes and drafts end when that tab closes; the app warns before leaving with unsent changes. Accepted server records remain available after signing in again.

Your choices

You can disable bill alerts, export records and delete your account. To correct bill information, edit it in the group or contact the group organizer. A person can be removed from a group once their balance is zero; their history remains, and a linked account loses access to that group. Archiving a group preserves its members' access to the records. For further privacy requests, email mail@merosplit.com.

We will update this page when how the app handles information changes.

Delete your MeroSplit account

In the app

  1. Open Me.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Read what will be removed and export anything you need. Type delete exactly, enter your current account email, then choose Delete account.

You need an internet connection. This deletes your user account, sign-in sessions, email, UPI address and notification registration. Your uploads, including bill attachments, are removed from visible records and marked for physical deletion. New download links are blocked immediately; previously issued links expire within five minutes unless physical deletion removes the file earlier. Authored bill text is removed or replaced with neutral placeholders, including in change history. Cached account records and queued changes are removed from this phone. Older phone records that you have not added to an account remain separate.

If you cannot use the app

Email mail@merosplit.com with the subject “MeroSplit account deletion”. Send from the account's email address where possible and say that you want your MeroSplit account deleted. We may ask you to confirm ownership. Do not send your password, UPI PIN, bank credentials or full payment-card details.

What can remain

Groups you own without another active linked member are removed. In groups with other members, your participant becomes “Deleted participant”; anonymous bill amounts, splits, dates and payment records remain so other members keep correct balances. Your authored text and uploaded attachments are removed from visible group records. If content added by someone else contains personal information you want removed, include that request in your email. Physical file deletion can remain pending if the storage provider is unavailable; new download links are blocked, existing links expire within five minutes, and physical deletion can be retried. Previously exported or downloaded copies held by other members are outside MeroSplit's control. This beta currently has no configured scheduled database backups or point-in-time recovery.

For a request to remove specific personal data without closing your account, email the same address and identify the data. Read the privacy policy.

Chat policy

Version 1. Accept this policy before your first message. Bill conversations are for respectful discussion between people with access to the bill.

Do not post abuse, harassment, threats, illegal material, sexual content, private credentials or another person's sensitive information. A small automated word/phrase filter rejects obvious examples; it does not detect all harmful content.

Use a message's menu to report a message or user, or block that person in chat. Blocking is reversible and affects your chat content and chat notifications only. It never changes shared bills, balances or group membership. Authorized support operators review reports and can hide abusive chat content.

Chat is stored on our server and is not end-to-end encrypted. Reports and message content may be reviewed by authorized support operators. Contact mail@merosplit.com for support, urgent abuse reports or personal-data requests.

Help with MeroSplit

Email mail@merosplit.com for help or beta feedback. Include your app version, phone model and a short description of what happened. Please use made-up bill details when sending screenshots, and never send a password or UPI PIN.

How balances work

Add what was paid and who shared it. MeroSplit works out what each person should pay or receive. “You are owed” means money is due to you. “You owe” means you need to pay someone back. The simpler repayment plan preserves each person's total while reducing payments within that group.

Groups and connected agents

Archived groups are under Archived groups. Leaving requires a settled balance and no unresolved reported payment or opening proposal. Shared records remain. An organizer passes responsibility to the next eligible joined member. Delete group removes your membership and the group from your list, including when there is no eligible successor. Shared financial history remains. Previously hidden groups can still be restored. Your saved drafts remain available for export. Connect an agent under Me → Connect MCP; setup instructions and Disconnect are available there.

Sync and notifications

Groups and People use the records saved in your account. Cached records remain available offline, and queued changes sync automatically when connected. If a change needs review, open it and compare your saved change with the latest record before retrying. Keep the app's data until your changes are accepted. New bill notices appear in your account immediately. Optional email follows after 10 minutes if the current bill is still unseen, even without the mobile app. Turn off bill alerts in Me → Notifications; phone notification settings can also block push. An unclaimed supplied email may receive an immediate generic notice with a Stop bill emails link. Contact save alone sends nothing.

Payment records

MeroSplit records money paid outside the app. Check the recipient and their UPI details in your payment app before paying. A recipient's acknowledgment is a record, not bank verification. Reversing a record updates balances; it does not reverse a bank transfer.

Your account and data

Sign in with the four-digit code sent to your email. Request a new code if it expires. Me has import, Export data and account controls. Export data downloads synced account history and work saved on this device together. Offline exports clearly indicate that server history is unavailable. Pending local file bytes are included up to 40 MB in total; synced files are represented by metadata. For account access help, contact support. Older records saved only on a phone require your confirmation before they are added to an account. You can request account or personal-data deletion even if the app has been uninstalled.
