How it works

  1. Add a bill

    Enter the amount, who paid and who shared it. Split equally, by exact amounts, by percentage or by shares. One bill can have more than one payer.

  2. See balances

    Each group shows who is owed and who owes. Simplify suggests payments that bring everyone's balance to zero.

  3. Record payments

    When someone pays back outside the app, record it and the balances update. A mistaken record can be reversed.

Keep a group for a trip or a shared flat, or a running tab with one person. Attach a photo or PDF to a bill; files are stored privately. On your phone or a supported regular browser, changes made offline sync when you reconnect. You can import a Splitwise CSV export after checking its preview, and export your account data whenever you need it.

Paying with UPI

If someone has added their UPI address, MeroSplit can open your payment app with it filled in. You check the details and approve the payment there.

MeroSplit does not send money, connect to your bank or check payments. Opening or returning from a payment app never marks anything as paid. You record the payment yourself.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open MeroSplit in your browser at merosplit.com/app and sign in with your email.

How are suggested payments worked out?

Simplify looks at every balance in the group and pairs the largest amounts first until each balance reaches zero. Nobody's total changes. A suggested payment is not tied to any one bill.

Can I fix a mistake?

Edit the bill, or reverse it and keep the original in history. Reversing a payment record updates balances. It does not reverse a bank transfer.

Who can see a group's bills?

The members of that group. The privacy policy explains invitations and sharing.

How do I delete my account?

Choose Delete account in Me, or follow the steps on the account deletion page.
